The woman behind Pure Spices

MADE WITH
A MOTHER'S CARE.

Before Pure Spices was a business, it was simply Tahrima making food for the people she loved.

BEFORE THERE WAS A BRAND,
THERE WAS HER KITCHEN.

Tahrima had already been making these foods for family and friends long before Pure Spices existed. People would take her food with them when returning abroad because they wanted the taste and freshness of what she made at home.

For years, she prepared these foods without charging anyone — it was simply what she did for the people she cared about.

SHE DIDN'T START
WITH A BUSINESS PLAN.

Pure Spices began in September 2020 during COVID-19. Tahrima was already making these foods regularly. She saw that people wanted authentic homemade Bengali foods, but the options available were often either expensive or simply did not have the same quality and freshness.

She decided to start making extra and selling it. The motivation was not simply business — she genuinely cares about people and wanted to make the food she loved available to others.

EVERYTHING STARTS
WITH WHAT SHE GROWS.

The ingredients used by Pure Spices come from Tahrima's own land, trees and properties. This includes coconut, date gur, sugarcane gur, mustard, chilies, spices and more.

Rather than buying finished ingredients from the market, Pure Spices relies heavily on what is grown and sourced directly through her own land and properties.

THE RECIPES
ARE HERS.

There is no factory recipe book. There is no corporate production line. The recipes are Tahrima's own — passed down through experience and instinct.

The food is made according to her own methods, taste and standards. Every batch reflects the way she has always made it for her family.

IF IT ISN'T GOOD ENOUGH
FOR HER FAMILY, IT DOESN'T LEAVE.

Tahrima personally checks every product before it is sent out. She only sends products that meet her standards.
