Where good food begins

IT STARTS
WITH THE INGREDIENT.

We believe the quality of the finished food can only be as good as what goes into it.

GROWN CLOSE TO HOME.

Many of the ingredients used by Pure Spices come from Tahrima's own land, trees and properties. This includes coconut, date gur, sugarcane gur, mustard, chilies, spices and more.

Rather than buying finished ingredients from the market, Pure Spices relies heavily on what is grown and sourced directly through her own land and properties.

NO MIDDLEMAN
BETWEEN CARE AND THE KITCHEN.

Pure Spices focuses on direct control over the ingredients and preparation process. Instead of relying on a large industrial production chain, Tahrima personally handles the preparation of her products.

From growing and selecting to preparing and packaging — everything stays within her hands and care.

OUR INGREDIENTS

Each one chosen, grown or prepared with care — because they're part of something bigger.

Coconut

Used in: Narkel Naru

Fresh coconut from Tahrima's own coconut trees, grated and prepared by hand.

Date Gur

Used in: Narkel Naru & sweets

Rich, naturally sweet gur that gives traditional Bengali sweets their characteristic depth.

Mustard

Used in: Oil, Achar, spices

A fundamental ingredient in Bengali cooking, valued for its distinctive aroma and sharp character.

Chilies

Used in: Powders, flakes, achar

Prepared for bold flavour and natural heat.

Turmeric

Used in: Turmeric powder, everyday cooking

A staple spice used throughout Bengali kitchens.

Coriander

Used in: Coriander powder, masala

Freshly prepared for aromatic depth.

Cumin

Used in: Jirar Gura, masala

A key spice in Bengali cooking, valued for its warm and earthy aroma.

Gur

Used in: Naru, moa, sweets

A traditional Bengali source of sweetness with a rich, deep flavour.

No machines are used in the production process. Everything is done by hand.

01

Ingredients are chosen based on quality, freshness and suitability.

02

03

NO PRESERVATIVES.
EVER.

Pure Spices products are made without preservatives. The focus is on good ingredients, careful preparation and proper storage.

IF IT DOESN'T MEET
HER STANDARD, IT DOESN'T GO OUT.

Tahrima personally inspects every product. She only sends products that meet her standards for freshness, quality, appearance, aroma, texture and overall condition.

This is not about certification. It is about care.

KEEP IT THE RIGHT WAY.

Storage instructions are provided on the packaging for each individual product. Follow the instructions specific to the product.

Up to 1 Year

When stored appropriately without freezing, products maintain their best quality.

Up to 3 Years

When kept in a freezer, products maintain their quality for longer.
