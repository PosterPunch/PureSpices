SOME FLAVOURS
FEEL LIKE HOME.

Bengali food is more than what is on the plate. It is the smell of spices being prepared, the sweetness of gur, the achar kept for later, and the recipes that become part of growing up.

FOOD IS PART OF
THE FAMILY STORY.

Many Pure Spices products are foods and ingredients that were naturally present in Tahrima's upbringing and family life. She grew up in Shailkupa, Bangladesh — deeply connected to her family and home.

Her family grew up together in a large family environment where food, preparation and sharing were part of everyday life.

মসলা

MASALA

Traditional Bengali cooking is built around carefully balanced spices. Pure Spices' masalas and spices are ground fresh and prepared in-house.

These aren't simply "spice products." They are ingredients used to create everyday Bengali food — the foundation of flavour in Bengali kitchens.

  • Turmeric
  • Chili Powder
  • Coriander Powder
  • Jirar Gura / Cumin Powder
  • Biriyani Masala
  • Mustard Seeds
  • Dried Red Chili Peppers
  • Chili Flakes
  • Mustard Oil
  • Beresta
কুমড়া বড়ি

KUMRA BORI

Kumra bori is a traditional Bengali preparation made to be enjoyed with everyday dishes. In Tahrima's family, it is traditionally enjoyed alongside foods such as fish curry and lau.

This is the kind of pairing that makes the product meaningful — not simply because it is old, but because it belongs to the everyday food memories of Bengali households.

গুড়

GUR

Pure Spices works with Akher Gur and Narikel Gur — deep sweetness, warm aroma, rich flavour and traditional Bengali character.

Pure Spices uses gur connected to Tahrima's own land and trees and properties.

Akher Gur

Date palm jaggery with deep, rich sweetness and a warm, earthy aroma.

Narikel Gur

Coconut jaggery with a familiar, comforting sweetness and traditional character.

নারকেল নাড়ু

NARKEL NARU

Tahrima uses coconut from her own coconut trees. The coconut is freshly grated. Date gur is used along with other ingredients to bring the mixture together.

The result is a traditional Bengali sweet made with the same care she has always used in her own kitchen.

মুড়ির মোয়া

MURIR MOA

Murir moa is a traditional Bengali homemade sweet and snack made using puffed rice and sweet ingredients such as gur. Pure Spices' version is made fresh and without preservatives.

The ingredients are prepared in-house. The focus is freshness, texture and the familiar taste of homemade Bengali food.

আচার

ACHAR

Pure Spices makes different varieties of Bengali achar. Whatever the craving, there is usually an achar for it.

Achar is part of the Bengali table as something that adds sharpness, sweetness, spice and character to a meal. Tahrima makes different types herself.
